I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), co-advised by Prof. Abeer Alwan and Prof. Yuan Tian. Before that, I received my M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from UCLA and my B.S. in Computer Science and Technology from Peking University.

I am broadly interested in speech processing and AI security, privacy, and safety, with a recent focus on the intersection between them.

I am seeking research internship opportunities in 2027. Please reach out if you have any leads.
