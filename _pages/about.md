I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), co-advised by [Prof. Abeer Alwan](https://www.ee.ucla.edu/abeer-alwan/) and [Prof. Yuan Tian](https://www.ytian.info/). Before that, I received my M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from UCLA and my B.S. in Computer Science and Technology from Peking University.

My research focuses on **spoken language processing** and **speech foundation models**. I am particularly interested in:

- **Speech tokenization** — discrete speech representations that balance acoustic fidelity and semantic information for unified speech understanding and generation.
- **Text-to-speech models** and generative speech modeling.
- **Post-training of Speech-LLMs and full-duplex speech models** — domain adaptation, parameter-efficient fine-tuning, and personalization of speech foundation models.
- **Speech privacy and AI security** — membership inference and training-data leakage in speech generation models, and trustworthy speech AI.

Recent highlights include a **Best Student Paper nomination at Interspeech 2026**, the **Best Paper Award at the AI4CSL Workshop @ IEEE ASRU 2025**, and selection as a **top-10 worldwide team in the Amazon Nova AI Challenge 2026**.

I am always happy to chat about speech, audio, and trustworthy AI — feel free to reach out by email.
